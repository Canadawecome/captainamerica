## Core Viral Bodycam Subtitle Techniques

### 1. **Speaker Labeling**
Use **specific roles/names** that give viewers instant context:
- ❌ Generic: "SPEAKER_1" / "COP"
- ✅ Specific: **OFFICER JENKINS** / **TRAVIS** / **DISPATCHER** / **WITNESS**

If names aren't known, use descriptive roles: **OFFICER** / **SUSPECT** / **BYSTANDER** / **SERGEANT**

---

### 2. **Word-by-Word Karaoke Highlighting**
Each word illuminates **precisely** when spoken. The active word gets:
- Brighter color (white/yellow vs gray base)
- Slight scale or glow
- Creates rhythm and pulls eyes through the sentence

---

### 3. **Emphasis Coloring**
Key stress words get **color coded**:
- 🟡 **Yellow**: Shock words, accusations, key actions (`TOUCHING`, `SEVERAL CALLS`, `DESCRIBE`)
- 🔴 **Red**: Escalation, profanity, threats, danger
- Keeps 70-80% white, highlights 20-30%

---

### 4. **Conversational Chunking**
- **2-5 seconds per chunk** (natural breath groups)
- Break at commas, pauses, or logical phrases
- Never split mid-thought
- Stack 1-2 lines max per frame

---

## Technical Implementation Notes

**Chunking rules applied:**
- Chunk 1: Complete accusatory statement (natural pause at "here.")
- Chunk 2: Follow-up question (clean sentence break)
- Chunk 3: Hesitation/filler (capture the human awkwardness)

**Emphasis strategy:**
- "SEVERAL CALLS" = gravity of situation
- "TOUCHING" = the taboo act
- "IMPULSE" = psychological question

**Styling (CapCut/Premiere):**
- Base text: `#F8FAFC` (off-white), 52-60pt bold sans
- Active word: `#FFFFFF` with subtle drop shadow
- Emphasis: `#FCD34D` (yellow) or `#EF4444` (red)
- Speaker tag: `#94A3B8` (slate-400), 24pt, top-left corner
- Background: Semi-transparent dark bar `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` behind text
- Animation: 80ms fade-in per word, slight scale (1.0 → 1.05) on emphasis words

This creates the **punchy, scannable, binge-worthy** feel that makes bodycam content go viral.
